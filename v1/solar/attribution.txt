The downloaded 2K textures for Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter,
Saturn and its rings, Uranus, Neptune, the Sun, and the Moon were resized
and re-encoded for the web. Lighting, atmospheric shading, orbital phases,
and the terrain on other moons and dwarf planets are Atlas visualizations.
The original texture publisher uses NASA elevation and imagery data and
fills gaps in certain maps with illustrative terrain.
