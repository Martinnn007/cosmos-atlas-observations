HYG Database v3.8, by David Nash (Astronexus).
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/blob/main/hyg/v3/hyg_v38.csv.gz

This adapted subset retains sources within 500 light-years. Columns contain HYG ID,
right ascension (hours), declination (degrees), distance (light-years),
apparent magnitude, spectral type, B−V color index, and available catalogue aliases.
Numeric precision has been rounded and missing B−V values use 0.65.
