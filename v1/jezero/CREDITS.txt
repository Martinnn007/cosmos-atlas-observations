Atlas horizontal terrain grid spacing is approximately 440–460 m
(HRSC), 130–145 m (CTX) and 4.7–5.0 m (HiRISE) before mesh resampling.
Coverage boundaries blend to the surrounding coarser observations.
Image colors blend HRSC color with the monochrome detail and are graded.
The vertical datum is the Mars areoid. The atmospheric scattering and
flight path are modeled. The global Mars surface follows solar asset credits.

Rover photo: PIA24264, Sol 3, NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS/ASU. 142 frames,
21 February 2021, Perseverance Mastcam-Z, Octavia E. Butler Landing.
The measured frame supplies the 360° horizontal view and the observed
foreground. Rendering outside the vertical photographic field uses
neutral edge shading. Display alignment is approximate.
