Atlas derivative
The 1.5 m/post stereo elevation and 0.5 m/pixel optical mosaic were
co-registered by the source team. Cosmos Atlas cropped and reprojected
both from Moon 2000 transverse Mercator to latitude/longitude. Elevations
are encoded as signed 16-bit meters relative to the Moon reference sphere
of radius 1,737.4 km, at approximately 13–14 m/post before mesh decimation.
The 4096 × 3072 orthomosaic is approximately 3 m/pixel and is tone-graded
for display. Its lighting contains shadows observed during acquisition.
Crop west/south/east/north = 30.445, 20.015, 30.902, 20.326 degrees.
Apollo 17 Challenger landing-site reference from the source team's map
is 20.19091°N, 30.77168°E. The Atlas camera path is a cinematic flyover.
It does not reconstruct the crew's descent trajectory.
