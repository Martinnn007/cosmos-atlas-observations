Reconstructed spacecraft position, attitude and Sun direction sampled every 0.5 s.
Enceladus body-fixed IAU_ENCELADUS. Altitude is the nearest distance to the
NAIF reference ellipsoid (256.6 × 251.4 × 248.3 km), not local terrain clearance.
UTC is the spacecraft event time, not the later Earth-received time.
The ISS wide-angle boresight and square 3.48° field are derived from the IK/FK.
The simulated scene uses archived pointing even between photos. Optical
distortion, exposure response, plume appearance and exterior framing are modeled.
