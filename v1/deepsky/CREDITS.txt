DESI EDR Visualization and Outreach Catalogue v1.0
https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/edr/vac/epoviz/
DESI Collaboration et al. (2024), The Early Data Release of the Dark Energy
Spectroscopic Instrument. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06308
Data license CC BY 4.0. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
License and official acknowledgments: https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/acknowledgments/

Changes: Atlas packs present-day comoving Cartesian positions and redshift
with quantization (1/1024 Glyr and 1/16000 redshift respectively), remaps the
up-axis, and colors the points by tracer type. The optional preview samples
one of every 14 entries per rosette, tracer and redshift bin. The footprint
guides summarize directions sampled in the public catalogue.

Pillars of Creation
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/pillars-of-creation/
Observation-constrained model by Leah Hustak and Ralf Crawford (STScI),
NASA’s Universe of Learning. Atlas voxelizes the positioning-reference STL.
Gas density, ionization colors and optical properties are modeled.
Spectroscopic context: https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/pillars-of-creation-revealed-in-3-d/

Sagittarius A*
Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration (2022), First Sagittarius A*
Event Horizon Telescope Results. I. The Shadow of the Supermassive Black
Hole in the Center of the Milky Way. https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ac6674
The Atlas uses the reported angular ring size (51.8 ± 2.3 microarcseconds)
as the stated apparent scale for its illustrative Schwarzschild optics.
Disk geometry, inclination, emission and colors are modeled.
